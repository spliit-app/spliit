# Getting Started with Spliit

Welcome to Spliit! This guide will help you get started with tracking and splitting expenses with your friends and family.

## What is Spliit?

Spliit is a minimalist web application designed to help you share expenses with friends and family. It's free, open-source, and respects your privacy - no ads, no account required (unless you want one for cross-device sync).

## Quick Start (5 minutes)

### 1. Create Your First Group

1. Visit [spliit.michiels.it](https://spliit.michiels.it)
2. Click **"Groups"** in the header or **"Create a Group"** on the homepage
3. Enter a group name (e.g., "Weekend Trip" or "Roommate Expenses")
4. Add participants:
   - Enter names of people in the group
   - Click **"Create Group"**
5. Done! Your group is ready

### 2. Add Your First Expense

1. In your group, click **"Add Expense"**
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Description**: What was purchased? (e.g., "Groceries")
   - **Amount**: How much was spent?
   - **Paid by**: Who paid for this expense?
   - **Split between**: Who should share the cost? (Select participant checkboxes)
3. Click **"Save"**

### 3. View Balances

- Navigate to the **"Balances"** tab
- See who owes whom and how much
- Spliit automatically calculates the most efficient way to settle debts

### 4. Share Your Group

Want others to join your group?

**Option 1: Share URL**
- Click the **Share** button
- Copy the URL and send it via text, email, or chat

**Option 2: QR Code** (New!)
- Click the **Share** button
- Select **QR Code**
- Others can scan with their camera to join instantly

## Key Features

### 📊 Track Expenses
- Add expenses as they happen
- Support for different currencies
- Upload receipts and images
- Add notes and categories

### ⚖️ Split Fairly
- **Equal split**: Divide costs evenly
- **Custom split**: Specify exact amounts or percentages
- **Unequal split**: Some people pay more than others
- **Exclude participants**: Not everyone has to share every cost

### 💸 Settle Balances
- See simplified debts (minimize number of transactions)
- Mark expenses as reimbursements
- Track who's paid back whom

### 🔒 Privacy First
- No signup required for basic use
- Data stored locally in your browser
- Optional account for cross-device access
- Open-source and transparent

## Do I Need an Account?

**No, you don't!** Spliit works completely anonymously.

**But you might want one if you:**
- Want to access groups on multiple devices
- Need to recover groups if you clear browser data
- Want to backup your expense history
- Prefer secure authentication (passphrase or passkey)

Learn more: [Account Authentication Guide](new-features.md#-account-authentication-secure--flexible)

## Tips for Success

### 1. Add Expenses Regularly
Don't wait until the end of the trip or month - add expenses as they happen to avoid forgetting

### 2. Use Categories
Categorize expenses (food, transport, accommodation) to see spending breakdown

### 3. Add Notes
Include details like "Dinner at Mario's" instead of just "Dinner" - future you will thank you

### 4. Take Photos
Upload receipt photos for record-keeping and proof of purchase

### 5. Review Before Settling
Check the balances together before anyone pays - it's better to catch mistakes early

### 6. Create Backups
For important groups (long trips, shared apartments), create regular backups

Learn more: [Backup & Restore Guide](new-features.md#-backup--restore-never-lose-your-data)

## Common Use Cases

### 🏖️ Group Trips
- Create a group for each trip
- Track shared costs (hotels, meals, activities)
- Settle up at the end

### 🏠 Shared Living
- Create a group for your household
- Track rent, utilities, groceries
- Monthly settlements

### 🎉 Events
- Birthday parties, weddings, group gifts
- Track contributions and expenses
- Share costs fairly

### 🍽️ Regular Outings
- Weekly dinners, coffee runs
- Keep a running tab with friends
- Settle periodically
